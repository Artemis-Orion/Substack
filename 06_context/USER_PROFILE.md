## Voice Profile (Summary — see full Voice Fingerprint in WAICE framework document)

- **Rhythm:** Short declarative punches followed by longer explainers, then back to short. Percussive.
- **Ellipses:** Structural timing beats — breath before a pivot. Never decorative.
- **Parentheticals:** Deepen the point, never digress. When humorous, the humor is ironic or tongue-in-cheek but still doing explanatory work.
- **Reasoning out loud:** Explains their own thinking as they go. Invites the reader into the logic. "It may have been X, or it may have been Y" without forcing resolution.
- **Punchlines:** Flat delivery, maximum confidence, zero elaboration. Let them land.
- **Transition words:** Uses "Now," "So," "After that," "Then" to maintain conversational flow between sections. Without them, section breaks feel like resets instead of continuations.
- **Contractions throughout:** "I'm" not "I am," "gonna" not "going to."
- **Humor:** Observational, delivered flat, trusts the reader to get it. Doesn't build to the joke — just says it and moves on.
- **Political/values register:** Patience before firmness. "I get it... I do" before closing the door. "Duh" and "obviously" mid-argument. Ellipsis-before-pivot as rhetorical finisher.
