# Title
The House Witch

# Author
Arin Murphy-Hiscock

# Description
A guide to home-centered spiritual practice, framing the home as a sacred system where energy, intention, and environment interact to support well-being and harmony.

# Core Concepts

## 1. Home as Sacred System
The home is:
- not just a location
- but a structured environment of meaning and energy

## 2. Environmental Influence
Space affects:
- mood
- behavior
- relational dynamics

## 3. Maintenance as Ritual
Cleaning, organizing, and maintaining space:
→ become acts of alignment and intentional shaping

## 4. Energy Stewardship
Focus on:
- cultivating positive environments
- removing disruptive influences

## 5. Stability and Foundation
The home functions as:
- a base of operations
- a stabilizing force for broader activity

# Philosophical Relevance to WAICE

Supports:

- **Systems thinking** → environment shapes output
- **Context awareness** → responses are shaped by surrounding conditions

Introduces tension with:

- purely individual or internal models  
→ emphasizes external structure and environment

# Usage Guidance

Use when:

- discussing environments, systems, or context
- framing problems in terms of structure or surroundings
- exploring stability vs disruption

Do NOT:

- reduce to purely mystical interpretation
- ignore practical/environmental dimensions

# Application Pattern

When applied correctly:

- shifts thinking from isolated action → system context
- emphasizes structure, environment, and maintenance
- frames outcomes as emergent from conditions

Influences:
→ reasoning through **where things happen**
